Two different diseases, not one

Standards specifications is a knowledge gap. Worst rate in the benchmark (29.8% of its rows wrong in both modes) and — decisively — 968 extra thinking tokens per question buy it +0.35pp. Reasoning cannot reconstruct a 3GPP clause the model never read. Research publications is a discrimination problem at scale. Its per-row rate is unremarkable (17.7%), but at 4,500 rows it still owns 40.4% of all errors, and thinking pays there (+2.78pp) — the knowledge is present, the selection is shaky.

What that split dictates

Two-thirds of hard-core errors in every subject are 5-choice questions — more near-miss distractors, more chances to be pulled off. The strategy follows directly: CPT feeds spec and paper text to close the Standards-spec knowledge hole, while thinking + preference calibration works the Research-publications mass. Lexicon needs nothing (9.6% rate, 3.0% of errors); the 1,000-row Standards-overview slice is the only place where a corpus gap (paywalled IEEE) still limits what any method can do.

The eval-time layer was measured shut before any training: prompt optimisation (DSPy seed → GEPA) scored −1.12pp on held-out — negative even with the full answer key leaked into the prompt (−0.45, i.e. zero exploitable headroom); self-consistency vote +0.7pp against an oracle of 83.85%; thinking +2.02pp overall but only +0.35 on the hardest subject.

Strategy in three sentences. (1) The model already knows the answer on most rows it misses — it loses to near-synonym distractors. (2) That skill is trainable: questions whose distractors differ by one release, one value, one sibling message name. (3) The raw material is public 3GPP text — and 96% of hard-core errors trace back to a specific spec file.

Pipeline · corpus → data → training → eval

The whole machine in seven steps

1

Source corpora — clean, verified

3GPP specs (error-density-ranked, 11,296 chunks) + Telco-Common-Corpus (22,178 telecom-filtered papers, ~400M tokens; 1.5B-token reserve). Contamination scan: 15/10,000 8-gram hits (noise). A third-party corpus shipping 9,986/10,000 test rows was caught and rejected.

2

Synthetic MCQ generation — self-hosted teacher

Qwen3.5-35B-A3B generates from chunks: 62,390 raw questions (specs) + literature batch. No external APIs touch benchmark-adjacent data.

3

Eight quality gates + student gate

Format/answerability/style gates keep 39,827 (63.8%); the student model then sits the exam — its real mistakes become the teaching signal. Literature adds 41,564 gated questions.

4

Training sets

train_v3.jsonl 40,730 (21,293 spec + 19,437 literature) · DPO pairs 25,080 fix + 24,700 anchors · CPT corpus ~200M tokens (error-targeted selection).

5

Training arcs

SFT (2 runs, failed) → single-token DPO (4 recipes, best +1.4 thinking) → CPT now running → GRPO-on-thinking planned.

6

Eval discipline

dev-1000 (400+600 stratified real rows) for all selection and diagnosis · held-out 9,000 frozen — exactly one final eval, for the final model only · never train on real test rows · A/A calibration before any cross-stack comparison.

7

Diagnosis loop

Every failed run is measured to death before the next move: dose-response, per-row flips, coverage-vs-fix correlation. The failures picked the current channel.

Decision tree · how each move was chosen

Six questions, each answered with a measurement

Nothing on this tree was decided by intuition. Every branch is a probe or an ablation that had to return a number before the next branch was allowed to open — which is why the dead ends are drawn here rather than deleted: they are what makes the surviving lane credible.

Diagnosis · why the ceiling exists

Fixes don't correlate with training data — the letter channel can't carry facts

The +15.8pp distractor probe put the knowledge partly inside the model; the flat fix-rate proves letter supervision cannot pull it out or add to it. Two channels remain: CPT writes facts into the weights; GRPO on thinking traces trains the selection process that harvests them. Closed-book rules exclude eval-time RAG.

Next moves

What happens after CPT

If CPT shows signal

  1. Scale dose from the 1.5B-token reserve corpus.
  2. Re-run the student gate on the CPT model → rebuild DPO pairs from its new confusions → thin calibration layer on top.
  3. GRPO on thinking traces (TRL is staged in venv-grpo): reward correct-and-well-formed answers over synthetic prompts — trains selection, closes the pass@k→pass@1 gap the 83.85% oracle exposed.

Standing discipline

  1. Held-out 9,000 stays frozen — one eval, final model only (pipeline staged: merge → vLLM → official harness config).
  2. Re-run all memorisation probes on any fine-tuned checkpoint before reporting.
  3. No letter-supervision scaling — the channel ceiling is measured, six times.
